About Me

I'm a Data Analytics professional with a background that's a little unconventional — and that's exactly what shapes how I approach problems. I hold a B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering, alongside a 5-year Fine Arts degree, giving me a mix of technical precision and a strong visual/design sensibility that shows up in how I build dashboards and reports.

Background

My journey has spanned full-stack (MERN) development, embedded systems, IoT, VLSI design, and UAV/drone systems — giving me a strong foundation in working with data, hardware, and systems thinking. I've also worked as a trainer, teaching Generative AI for Design & Media Innovation to BCA and BSc students, which sharpened my ability to break down complex technical concepts simply.

Why Data Analytics

Across all these experiences, the common thread was always the same — working with data, finding patterns, and turning them into something clear and actionable. That's what pulled me toward Data Analytics, and why I've focused on building dashboards and analysis projects using Power BI, SQL, Tableau, Excel, and Python.
